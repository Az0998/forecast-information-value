Dear Editor,

Please consider the research article

“Nested gauges are not lumped rainfall–runoff: a regime model of information value on operational river networks”

for publication in Hydrology and Earth System Sciences.

The paper is not a new encoder and not a CAMELS rainfall–runoff long short-term memory study. Nested operational gauges are a different object from lumped precipitation-to-discharge catchments. We estimate a regime of information value — when lagged-upstream linear routing already saturates next-day skill, when an extra gauge still helps, when unused catchment rain still has a 3-day ceiling, and when flood critical success index disagrees with Nash–Sutcliffe efficiency — on 21 documented USGS and Water Survey of Canada nested networks with block-bootstrap uncertainty.

We confirm this is not a dual submission. A companion missingness study (Mask-View) is a separate manuscript. Code and frozen tables: https://github.com/Az0998/forecast-information-value

Suggested subject areas: catchment hydrology; floods and flood forecasting; hydrological modelling.

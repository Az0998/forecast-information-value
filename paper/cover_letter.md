Please consider our manuscript

“Information bottlenecks, not architectures: a cross-media protocol for mid-range environmental forecasts”

for publication in Ecological Informatics.

The manuscript is not another LSTM-on-a-basin or Transformer-on-a-shelf case study. It proposes a four-part evaluation grammar (domain-simple baseline, event score, information ceiling, failure locus) and shows that the same grammar yields transferable conclusions on five climate-contrasting rivers and an East China Sea shelf oxygen cube: learned models add decision skill only while the limiting input is present (usable precipitation after day 1; dense oxygen history at 1 month). We report where linear routing beats deep models (semi-arid Verde), where climatology should replace the Transformer (oxygen lead ≥ 2 months), and where residuals concentrate (coastal shelf). Code and frozen tables are at https://github.com/Az0998/forecast-information-value.

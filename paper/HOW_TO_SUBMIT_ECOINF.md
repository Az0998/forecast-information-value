# How to submit (Ecological Informatics)

Journal: *Ecological Informatics* (Elsevier) · article type: Research paper  
Guide: https://www.elsevier.com/journals/ecological-informatics/1574-9541/guide-for-authors  
Portal: Elsevier Editorial Manager (search “Ecological Informatics”)

Submit only to *Ecological Informatics* for this manuscript.  
Do not submit the Mask-View sister paper to the same journal at the same time.  
Do not target AIES until the oxygen cube is replaced by GOBAI-O2.

## Files to upload

| EM field | Local file |
|---|---|
| Manuscript | `paper/EcoInf_information_value_manuscript.docx` |
| Highlights | paste `paper/highlights.txt` (5 bullets, each ≤85 characters) |
| Cover letter | `paper/cover_letter.txt` (plain text; `cover_letter.md` is the same text) |
| Suggested reviewers | enter from `paper/suggested_reviewers.md` (search EM/ORCID; do not guess emails) |
| Data availability | https://github.com/Az0998/forecast-information-value |

Figures and tables are already embedded in the Word file. No separate graphical abstract is required.

## Editorial declarations

- Single author; no competing interests; no external funding.
- Oxygen cube is WOA-informed (climatology + synthetic anomalies), not GOBAI-O2.
- Companion Mask-View paper is in preparation; not a dual submission of the same claim.

## Before upload

1. Confirm https://github.com/Az0998/forecast-information-value shows the latest commit (Bow tables, OLS ceiling, Figs. 2–5, Word file).
2. If the public repo is behind this folder, commit and `git push origin main` first. Do not submit on a 404 data URL.
3. In EM reviewer fields, search ORCID/name; do not invent emails.
4. Graphical abstract is optional under Elsevier Your Paper Your Way. Skip unless the portal requires one.
